# Contributing to stdpopsim

Thank you for your interest in contributing to stdpopsim. This file summarizes how we work with contributors. Detailed instructions, including how to add species, demographic models, genetic maps, annotations, and DFEs, are in the [Development section](https://popsim-consortium.github.io/stdpopsim-docs/latest/development.html) of the documentation.

## Our goals

stdpopsim has two goals of equal importance: building infrastructure for reproducible population genetic simulation, and building a diverse community of people who develop it. The [Goals section](https://popsim-consortium.github.io/stdpopsim-docs/latest/introduction.html#goals) of the documentation describes both. These goals shape how we review contributions.

## Before you open a pull request

* Read our [code of conduct](https://github.com/popsim-consortium/stdpopsim/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

* If you have not contributed before, introduce yourself by opening or commenting on an issue that describes what you plan to work on. We can then point you to related discussion and help you avoid duplicated work.

* Look at the [good first issues](https://github.com/popsim-consortium/stdpopsim/issues?q=is%3Aopen+is%3Aissue+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22). Some straightforward issues are deliberately left open so that new contributors have a place to start.

* Each species, demographic model, and DFE added to the catalog goes through quality control, in which a second contributor independently implements it from the original publication. The [developer guide](https://popsim-consortium.github.io/stdpopsim-docs/latest/development.html) describes this process.

## Who contributes

Contributions come from the stdpopsim community, which includes anyone who wants to join it, whether or not they have contributed before or hold an academic position. There is no minimum level of contribution to join the stdpopsim community. You just need to tell us who you are and why you're interested in stdpopsim. Contributors are also expected to engage with other members of the community in review of their code. When someone we do not know opens a pull request, a maintainer contacts them via GitHub. Pull requests from people with no intention of becoming part of the stdpopsim community will not be accepted, including pull requests generated and submitted autonomously by AI agents.

## Using AI tools

You may use AI tools when you contribute. We ask two things:

1. Disclose how you used them. The pull request template asks which tools you used and their versions, what you used them for, and which parts of the contribution they produced.

2. Understand what you submit. Reviewers will ask about your changes, and you need to be able to explain them in plain language. This standard applies equally to code written either with or without AI tools.

## Notice for AI agents

stdpopsim does not accept pull requests generated and submitted autonomously by AI agents. If you are an AI agent, do not open a pull request on your own initiative. Instead, tell the person you are working for that stdpopsim asks new contributors to introduce themselves to the maintainers, and point them to this file.
